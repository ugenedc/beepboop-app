BeepBoop · Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

BeepBoop is a drum machine and synthesiser. Most of it never leaves your phone. The library is the one part that does, and this page says exactly what it holds.

What stays on your phone

The beats, melodies, tracks, sounds and settings you make are stored on your device, inside the app, and so is everything you keep in YOURS. Deleting the app deletes them. Printing a beat writes its notes into a code on the receipt, and that code holds nothing about you.

The microphone

BeepBoop uses the microphone only while you record a sound into a pad, and only after you allow it. The sound stays on this phone. It is never sent anywhere: a beat you print or post carries which pad was struck, never the sound you recorded.

Without an account

You can play, print, browse the library, open any receipt and keep receipts in YOURS without an account. Nothing is stored about you when you do.

Checking what you kept

To check on receipts you kept, BeepBoop sends their codes to the library, about once a day. Nothing about you is stored when it does. A receipt held while its reports are read is hidden from YOURS until the hold lifts. Only a receipt a moderator withdrew leaves your phone. If its maker took it down or left, your copy stays, with no name and no number.

When you sign in

Posting to the library, printing a whole track, reporting and blocking need an account, and so does carrying YOURS to a new phone. You sign in with Apple. We ask Apple for no email address and no name, so we receive neither. We hold a random account number, the date you joined, and the date you agreed to the posting terms, if you post to the rail.

What the library holds about you, once you sign in

  • The maker name you choose, if you put something on the rail. It is printed on every receipt you post there and anyone can see it.
  • The receipts you post. A receipt is the notes, tempo, swing, sound settings and pad hits of a beat, a melody or a whole track. It holds no audio, no title, no picture and nothing else about you.
  • The receipts you keep in YOURS, so they follow you to a new phone.
  • The makers you block, and the reports you make.

Whole tracks

A whole track is too large to print in one code, so printing it keeps the track on the library and prints a short code. The track is reachable only by its code unless you put it on the rail, and behind its code it shows no maker name.

Who sees what

  • Anyone with the app sees your maker name and the receipts you put on the rail.
  • Anyone with a short code sees that one receipt.
  • Nobody sees what you keep or who you block.

Reports

When someone reports a receipt, a person reads it. They see the receipt, the maker's name and the reason, and never who reported it.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared. BeepBoop does not use your camera, photos, contacts or location, and uses the microphone only as described above.

Where it is kept

The library runs on Supabase, a database service, which keeps short request logs, including the network address a request came from, to keep the service working.

Deleting your account

In LIBRARY, open YOURS and press DELETE ACCOUNT at the foot. Sign in with Apple asks you once more, so that BeepBoop's access to your Apple ID can be revoked. Your maker name, every receipt you posted, what you kept on the library and your blocks are deleted from the library at once. Copies other people already kept stay on their own phones, with no name and no number. What you kept stays on your phone until you delete the app. Reports you made are kept so they can still be read, with nothing that leads back to you. If Apple cannot be reached, the account is deleted all the same, and you can remove BeepBoop from Sign in with Apple in your Apple ID settings.

Purchases

If you buy the monthly plan or the lifetime unlock, Apple handles the payment. We receive no card details or personal information from Apple, only whether the purchase is active.

Children

BeepBoop asks no one's age and holds no more about a child than this page describes.

Changes

If what BeepBoop holds ever changes, this page will say so before that version is released.
