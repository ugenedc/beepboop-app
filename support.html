BeepBoop · Support

Questions, problems or ideas about BeepBoop: write to eugene@themostvaluableproduct.com. A person reads every message.

Your plan

The monthly plan and the lifetime unlock are bought through the App Store. To restore a purchase on a new device, swipe to STORE and press RESTORE.

Cancel

Cancel the monthly plan at least 24 hours before it renews, in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Or swipe to STORE and press MANAGE.

Posting to the library

Before your first post you agree to the posting terms. Everyone can hear what you post, with your maker name on it. Post beats you made, or that you have the right to share. There is no tolerance for objectionable posts or abusive makers: no hate, harassment, sexual content, violence, threats, or copies of someone else's beat.

Reports and blocking

To report a receipt or block its maker, hear it in LIBRARY and touch its flag. A person reads every report. A post that breaks the posting terms comes down, and a maker who breaks them is barred. If you think a post of yours came down by mistake, write to the address above with its number.

Your recordings

A sound you record into a pad stays on your phone. A beat you print or post carries the pad, never the recording.

Your account

To delete your account, swipe to LIBRARY, open YOURS and press DELETE ACCOUNT at the foot. What you posted and kept on the library is deleted at once; what is on your phone stays.
