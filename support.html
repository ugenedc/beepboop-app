Your plan

The monthly plan and the lifetime unlock are bought through the App Store. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the PLAN bench and press RESTORE.

Cancel

Cancel the monthly plan at least 24 hours before it renews, in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Or open the PLAN bench and press MANAGE.

Privacy

BeepBoop collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
